The detail that sets us apart

Mitered edges, no metal trim

On corners, niches and shower curbs we cut each tile at a precise 45° angle so two pieces meet in a clean, seamless edge. No plastic or metal trim strips, just the tile itself, wrapping the corner like solid stone.
